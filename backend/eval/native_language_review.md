# Native-language review required

Hindi and Telugu number-word rules in `app/meaning/number_parser.py` have not been reviewed by native speakers. Spoken mappings are labeled `inferred`; Telugu mappings are `provisional` and require speaker confirmation. Please review the parser vocabulary, colloquial fractions, units and utterances in `utterances.jsonl` before relying on linguistic mappings for financial or transactional use. No mapping should be described as native-verified until the reviewer, date, and reviewed cases are recorded here.

The 100 utterance rows are a text corpus only. They do not contain recordings, reference transcripts from real speech, provider outputs, latency measurements, or human ratings. Fill those from actual runs in `results.csv` before reporting any metric.
